MAN + SHOPPING CART

Open man_shopping_cart.blend for the complete posing rig.
Select RIG and enter Pose Mode (Ctrl+Tab).
MASTER: move/rotate the entire assembly.
CART: move/rotate the cart; both hands follow the handle via IK.
pelvis: move the body; feet stay on their IK controls.
CTRL_foot.L / .R: animate steps; rotate to tilt shoes.
CTRL_hand.L / .R: adjust hand grips relative to the cart.
wheel_front/rear.L/R: rotate LOCAL Y to roll the wheels.
Enable DEFORM bone collection to pose spine, head and fingers.
Optional pole bones are provided but not assigned; the initial bends guide IK.
Character uses intentionally stylized rigid segments with rounded joints.

FBX contains meshes, materials and connected skeleton. Blender IK constraints
and control behavior are native to the .blend and are not portable in FBX.
Animate in the .blend, then export FBX with Bake Animation enabled.
No animation clips have been added. Original cart.blend is preserved.
